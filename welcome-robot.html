PROJECT 08 // WELCOMEBOT

WELCOME
ROBOT

EXPO SUCCESSFUL

"I wanted to build a robot that doesn't simply stand at an expo — I wanted it to actually welcome people."

IDEA→ DESIGN→ CIRCUIT→ CODE→ BODY→ TEST→ EXPO

A foam-bodied expo greeter robot that waves hello with a servo-driven arm, blinks through animated LED-matrix eyes, and plays a welcome jingle — all triggered by two push buttons on its back panel. The head was later redesigned into a compact dual-matrix version that scrolls "WELCOME" and my name across its two 8×8 displays before looping into circle-and-square eye animations.
