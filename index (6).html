#001Welcome Robot
EXPO READY
LOADING 3D MODEL…
DRAG TO ROTATE · SCROLL TO ZOOM

A foam-bodied expo greeter robot built to welcome visitors at my project stall. It waves hello with a servo-driven arm, blinks through animated LED-matrix eyes, and plays a welcome jingle — all triggered by two push buttons on its back panel. The head was later redesigned into a compact dual-matrix version that scrolls "WELCOME" and my name across its two 8×8 displays before looping into circle-and-square eye animations.

FEATURES
  • Servo-driven right arm waves a 70° hand salam on greeting
  • 3× 8×8 LED matrix eyes with expressive blink animation
  • DFPlayer Mini + speaker for welcome voice line & songs
  • Two rear push buttons — one for greeting, one for music
  • Rev B head: dual-matrix scroll text "WELCOME" + name, looping into circle/square eye animation
  • Flexible ball-joint neck on the redesigned head
CORE PARTS

  • DFPlayer Mini
  • 3× 8×8 LED Matrix (MAX7219)
  • SG90 / MG90S Servo Motor
  • 3W–5W Speaker
  • 2× Push Buttons
  • MicroSD card (MP3 files)
  • 5V 2A power supply
  • 5mm foam board / PVC sheet body
  • Hot glue, acrylic paint & stickers
